Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect the information you share with us through apply-mccollege.pk.

Last updated: {{ current_year }}

{# ============================ Policy content ============================ #}

This admissions portal is operated by MC College of Medical & Allied Health Sciences, affiliated with Jinnah Sindh Medical University (JSMU). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal information you provide when enquiring about admission. This policy explains what we collect, why we collect it, and the choices you have.

{# --- 1. Information we collect --- #}

1 Information We Collect

When you submit an admission enquiry through this website, we collect the information you choose to provide:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address (optional)
  • Your city
  • The program you are interested in
{# --- 2. How we use it --- #}

2 How We Use Your Information

The information you provide is used only for admission counseling — so our team can contact you, answer your questions, and guide you through the application process for your chosen program. We do not use it for any unrelated purpose.

{# --- 3. Sharing --- #}

3 How We Share Your Information

Your information may be shared internally with our admission staff so they can follow up on your enquiry. We never sell your personal information to any third party, and we do not share it for marketing or advertising by others.

{# --- 4. Cookies & analytics --- #}

4 Cookies & Analytics

We may use cookies and analytics tools to understand how visitors use this website and to improve the experience. These help us measure traffic and page performance. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some parts of the site may not work as expected.

{# --- 5. Data retention & deletion --- #}

5 Your Choices & Data Deletion

You are in control of your information. You can request that we delete the personal information we hold about you at any time by emailing us at the address below. Once we verify your request, we will remove your information from our records, except where we are required to retain it by law.

{# --- 6. Contact --- #}

Contact Us

For any privacy questions or to request deletion of your information, email us at:

admissions@mccollege.edu.pk
